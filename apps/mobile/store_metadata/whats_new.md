# U Learn — What's New

**Version:** 1.0.22 (build 24)  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

## English (en-US)

Whiteboard lessons play more smoothly in courses — ink and shapes appear as the teacher drew them. Teachers can add rectangles on the board, and board lessons use a larger viewing area for easier study.
